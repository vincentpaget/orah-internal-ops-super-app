Global Rules

Keep these fields up to date on every open opp, at every stage.

Close Date
When the customer will sign, not when we'd like them to. Keep it in line with the confirmed timeline and move it as soon as it slips.
Amount
An accurate estimate of the Net ARR. Update it whenever the scope changes. By Proposal it should match the offer.
Stage
The furthest stage where every criterion above is met. Update it the day that changes, forwards or backwards.
Type
Upsell, Cross-sell, Price Increase, Contraction, Churn Risk or Churn Notice Given. Set it at Qualifying and change it if the deal changes.
Next Step
A mutual, dated step agreed with the customer that moves the deal forward. Write it as date: what happens, with whom. Update it after every customer touch.
